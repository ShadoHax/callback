# Callback demo options

These are alternative stories for the same two people: **Scanner** notices something; **Friend** receives and answers a message on their own phone. The current M1/V3 prop kit is **one readable *War and Peace* cover, one clearly labeled coffee bag or cup, and two phones**. The M1/V3 source lines are loaded as eight labeled fictional messages; the other source lines below are proposals, not imported conversations. A printed cover or rendered product image is an **image proxy**, not a physical-item or phone-camera result. The strongest presentation keeps the connection useful even when no one buys anything.

## What is ready versus proposed

- **M1 and V3 are implemented and hosted.** `inspired` carries Maya's verbatim writing ambition; `prefers` carries her coffee taste and avoid words. The book opens an editable invitation; the coffee scan offers a buyer-chosen gift from the labeled demo coffee catalog. Hosted checks used rendered images, fictional chats, and scripted replies. The two-phone human exchange and phone checkout still need rehearsal. See [PHONE_PRACTICE.md](PHONE_PRACTICE.md).
- The current catalog has six fictional coffees and **no book, pen, notebook, snack, or refill**. Exact-item/family shopping remains in the rules and test fixture; the V3 coffee gift is marked **related** to the photo's item kind, never the same packet seen in the photo.
- A gift from an active `wanted`/`asked_to_find` relation, a buyer-chosen gift from a `prefers` taste, and a shared-item path from `planned_together` are implemented. A book-to-pen or notebook gift based on a writing ambition is not. Friend's words establish relevance; **Scanner's explicit checkout approval** is the buyer's authority to spend.
- Call any proposed merchant, stock, price, tax, or delivery detail **demo fixture data** unless independently verified. Stripe TEST is not a Visa API or Visa Intelligent Commerce integration. No flow authorizes an automatic purchase.

## Two-minute stage scripts

**Recommended Meta script (M1, two phones, no checkout).** 0:00–0:15: “My friend said *War and Peace* inspired a novel idea, and I wanted to follow up.” 0:15–0:40: hold its readable cover still; show the confirmed Maya card and open her exact source line. 0:40–1:05: edit and explicitly send “Saw *War and Peace* and thought of your novel. How's it going?” 1:05–1:35: Maya reads it on the other phone and types a real reply. If she welcomes it, Alex offers to read a first page or meet for a short writing session. 1:35–2:00: point to the reply and explain that the image supplied the cue, Maya's fictional, labeled source line supplied the reason, and both people chose what to say. If the actual cover is unreliable on a phone, show a labeled successful rehearsal recording or use a manual photo and say so.

**Recommended Visa script (V3).** 0:00–0:15: hold up clearly labeled coffee; show Maya's stated light-roast whole-bean taste. 0:15–0:40: open the gift options, confirm the editable $25 all-in budget, show Ethiopia Yirgacheffe first, and read the ground-coffee tradeoff and dark-roast exclusions. 0:40–1:00: review the $23.11 demo total, explicitly approve Stripe TEST checkout, complete its hosted steps, and show the paid TEST receipt if the phone run succeeds. Explain that Alex chose the gesture and Maya did not ask for this packet. Label the merchant and prices as demo fixtures, and report any phone failure honestly.

## Rehearsal matrix for the chosen pair

| Case | Expected result |
|---|---|
| Readable hero prop, current friend-authored source, mapped Friend | Correct person and relation; source review precedes any message or purchase. |
| Blurry cover, different book title, unrelated object, or absent source | Clarification or no unsupported connection; no confident invented relation. A different printing of the same book can still be the same work. |
| Friend changes their mind, a plan is canceled, or a preference is stale | Old invitation or gift reason is removed or clearly marked unusable after memory rebuild. |
| Scanner edits or abandons message; Friend replies freely | Nothing sends without Scanner's action; Friend's actual reply appears and can change the plan. |
| Preference missing or conflicting, or shopper budget too low | Check whether the gift purpose is offered and whether each option has a supported taste reason. Record weak or conflicting matches as failures for follow-up; budget exclusions include shipping and tax. |
| Item differs from photo, is unavailable, or has an unverified compatibility claim | Accurate identity and tradeoff; no misleading “same item” or purchasable option. |
| Quote changes, checkout is canceled/expired/failed, or callback is delayed | Fresh review when needed, no false paid receipt or duplicate charge, verified paid state only after provider confirmation. |
| Camera permission denied or live scan fails | Clearly labeled manual photo/upload fallback; report the actual phone result rather than a claimed live success. |

Run the chosen story end to end on the actual two phones and record successes **and** misses with a denominator.
