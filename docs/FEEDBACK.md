# Early feedback protocol (3–5 people)

Purpose: early usability evidence. Is a connection accurate, specific, and welcome, and does a real exchange follow? This is not evidence of lasting relationship change.

**Setup.** Use each participant's own consented stories if possible; otherwise the labeled synthetic set, and say so. Write down whether the scenario was arranged (we chose the object and story) or found (they brought an object). Don't coach. Record failures as carefully as successes.

**After the scan (scanner):**
1. Was the person and reason accurate? (yes / partly / no; what was wrong)
2. Was it specific to this relationship, or could it apply to anyone?
3. Would you actually send it? What did you change in the message?
4. When, without our prompting, would you open this app? ("never" is a valid answer.)

**After receiving (recipient):**
5. Was this welcome to receive? Did seeing your own earlier words help or feel intrusive?
6. Did you reply with a real next step? (quote it if they agree)

**Shopping continuation (optional):**
7. Did seeing who owns or tried it change what you'd do? Would you ask them?
8. Were the options and reasons understandable? Did anything feel pushy?
